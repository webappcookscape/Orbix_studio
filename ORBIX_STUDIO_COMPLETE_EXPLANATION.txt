1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
Orbix Studio (internally referred to as Cupboard CAD) is an enterprise-grade,
browser-based interior design CAD and commercial quotation platform tailored for
custom residential cabinetry, modular kitchens, wardrobes, and space planning.

The platform bridges three critical domains in real time:
1. 2D Floor Plan Design:
   High-precision interactive canvas drafting tool with millimeter snapping,
   wall creation, room polygon discovery, door/window placement, blueprint
   underlay calibration, and architectural segmented dimension chains.
2. 3D Parametric Cabinetry & Interior Studio:
   Real-time WebGL rendering via Three.js. Automatically extrudes 3D walls and
   rooms from 2D floor plans, parametric carcass generation, door swings,
   internal shelving, drawer boxes, PBR materials (woodgrains, laminates,
   fluted glass, brass/matte hardware), and lighting.
3. Commercial BOQ (Bill of Quantities) & Quotation Engine:
   Instant calculation of square footage (SFT), carcass materials, shutter
   finishes, hardware components, factory labor, installation, freight, margin,
   and 18% GST with locked versions and SketchUp / CSV export capabilities.
4. Cloud Collaboration & Multi-User Management:
   PostgreSQL-backed layered Express + Prisma ORM backend with cookie-based
   persistent sessions, role-based project sharing (Viewer/Editor/Admin),
   project cloning (forking), and admin employee management.

3.1 orbix-core.js — The Geometric and Commercial Heart
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Location: d:\CookScape workspace\Project_files\Orbix_studio\orbix-core.js
Lines of Code: ~4,000+
Dependencies: Independent (works in both Browser and Node.js via UMD module wrapper).

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
1. 2D Vector Mathematics (`Core.Vec2`):
   - `dist(a, b)`: Euclidean distance $\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$.
   - `add(a, b)`, `sub(a, b)`, `scale(a, s)`, `normalize(v)`.
   - `dot(a, b)`: Scalar dot product $a_x b_x + a_y b_y$.
   - `cross(a, b)`: 2D determinant $a_x b_y - a_y b_x$.
   - `pointInPolygon(pt, poly)`: Ray-casting winding algorithm checking if a point
     lies inside an arbitrary 2D room boundary.
   - `polygonCentroid(poly)`: Geometric center calculation used for room labeling.
   - `polygonArea(poly)`: Shoelace theorem computing exact square millimeters.

2. Wall & Geometry Modeling:
   - `createWall(options)`: Instantiates a wall with start {x,y}, end {x,y},
     thickness (default 150mm), height (default 2800mm), and unique ID (`wall_...`).
   - `wallLength(w)`: Length of wall vector.
   - `wallDirection(w)`: Normalized unit vector $(end - start) / length$.
   - `wallNormal(w)`: Perpendicular vector $(-dir_y, dir_x)$.
   - `wallPolygon(w)`: Returns 4 corners in counter-clockwise order forming the
     2D rectangular wall body with specified thickness.
   - `splitWall(project, wallId, splitPt)`: Splits a wall into two connected wall
     segments while recalculating and reassigning existing doors and windows.

3. Openings Model (Doors & Windows):
   - `createOpening(options)`: Manages doors and windows. Tracks `wallId`, `offset`
     (distance from wall.start to opening center), `width` (e.g. 800mm, 900mm),
     `height` (e.g. 2100mm), `swing` ('in-left', 'in-right', 'out-left', 'out-right'),
     and `sillHeight` (for windows, default 900mm).

4. Room Detection Algorithm (`detectRooms(walls)`):
   - Converts the undirected wall graph into a planar planar straight-line graph (PSLG).
   - Detects all minimal counter-clockwise closed cycles (faces).
   - Filters out the infinite exterior outer boundary.
   - Computes square meters and square feet for each detected interior room.

5. Commercial Quotation & BOQ Engine:
   - SFT Formula: $\text{Width (mm)} \times \text{Height (mm)} / 92,903.04$.
   - Applies the configured rate per SFT (e.g. ₹1,700/SFT for Essential,
     ₹2,200/SFT for Premium, ₹2,800/SFT for Luxury).
   - Computes:
     * Woodwork Base Subtotal
     * Hardware & Accessories
     * Factory Labor Charges (~8.41%)
     * Installation Charges
     * Freight & Logistics
     * Commercial Margin
     * GST (18% Goods & Services Tax)
     * Grand Total (Rounded to nearest rupee)

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS & ALGORITHMIC ENGINES
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Square Footage (SFT) & Dimensional Conversion Formula
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the interior design and modular cabinetry industry in India, furniture units are
quoted in square feet (SFT) based on their frontal elevation dimensions:

$$\text{Area}_{\text{SFT}} = \frac{\text{Width (mm)} \times \text{Height (mm)}}{92,903.04}$$

*Why 92,903.04?*
- 1 foot = 304.8 mm
- 1 square foot = $304.8 \times 304.8 = 92,903.04 \text{ mm}^2$

Example Verification:
A wardrobe measuring 1200 mm width by 2000 mm height:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1200 \times 2000}{92903.04} = \frac{2,400,000}{92903.04} \approx 25.8334 \text{ SFT}$$

At ₹1,700/SFT:
$$\text{Cost} = 25.83337 \times 1700 = \text{₹}43,916.75$$

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 Commercial Quotation & Labor Matrices
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Orbix Studio calculates the full bill of quantities through an eight-stage commercial formula:
1. `Woodwork Subtotal` = $\sum (\text{Unit SFT} \times \text{Material Rate})$
2. `Hardware Subtotal` = Hinges, telescopic drawer channels, handles, hanging rods.
3. `Net Base Amount` = $\text{Woodwork Subtotal} + \text{Hardware Subtotal}$
4. `Factory Labor & Fabrication` = $\text{Net Base Amount} \times 8.41\%$
5. `Installation Charges` = $\text{Net Base Amount} \times 5.00\%$
6. `Freight & Logistics` = Fixed or distance-weighted charge (~₹1,500)
7. `Net Commercial Subtotal` = $\text{Net Base} + \text{Labor} + \text{Installation} + \text{Freight}$
8. `GST (18%)` = $\text{Net Commercial Subtotal} \times 0.18$
9. `Grand Total` = $\text{Math.round}(\text{Net Commercial Subtotal} + \text{GST})$

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 Room Polygon Detection (Face Finding & Shoelace Formula)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When walls are drawn, `Core.detectRooms()` discovers closed spaces automatically:
1. Build adjacency list of all connected wall vertices.
2. Sort adjacent edges counter-clockwise around every vertex by angle $\theta = \text{atan2}(dy, dx)$.
3. Trace smallest counter-clockwise cycles (left-hand rule).
4. Compute signed area using the Shoelace Formula:
   $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} (x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i) \right|$$
5. Discard negative/clockwise outer hulls; keep all positive interior cycles as rooms.
6. Compute room centroid for 2D badge placement:
   $$C_x = \frac{1}{6A} \sum (x_i + x_{i+1})(x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i)$$
   $$C_y = \frac{1}{6A} \sum (y_i + y_{i+1})(x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i)$$

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4 Segmented Architectural Dimensions & Exterior Normal Selection
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To place dimension lines on the OUTSIDE of rooms:
1. For any wall $W$ with direction $\vec{d} = (end - start) / L$, compute normal $\vec{n} = (-d_y, d_x)$.
2. Sample test points $P_{\text{pos}} = \text{mid} + \vec{n} \times 120\text{mm}$ and $P_{\text{neg}} = \text{mid} - \vec{n} \times 120\text{mm}$.
3. If $P_{\text{pos}}$ is inside a detected room and $P_{\text{neg}}$ is outside, invert normal $\vec{n} = -\vec{n}$.
4. Partition wall $0 \dots L$ into ordered divisions $[0, t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}, L]$.
5. Segment 1: from $0 \dots t_{\text{start}}$ (labeled with Arrow 1 $\uparrow$ toward Corner A).
6. Opening: width $t_{\text{end}} - t_{\text{start}}$ (e.g. 800 mm).
7. Segment 2: from $t_{\text{end}} \dots L$ (labeled with Arrow 2 $\downarrow$ toward Corner B).
8. Draw perpendicular dashed witness lines from wall face out to dimension line, with
   solid circular dots at intersections and dimension pill badges.
